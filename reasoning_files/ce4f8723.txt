  - Even when the top cell is 0, the output is still 3 whenever the corresponding cell in the lower 4×4 block (the one after the all-4s separator) is non-zero (i.e., 2).
  - The row of 4s in the middle is just a separator and should be ignored.

  - Look at the top cell at (r, c).
  - Also look at the cell five rows below it at (r+5, c) in the bottom block.
  - If either of those two cells is non-zero, put 3 in the output; otherwise put 0.

  - A small helper that returns true if grid[r][c] is non-zero or grid[r+5][c] is non-zero.
  - A row function that maps a given r over c=0..3 and turns that boolean into 3 or 0.
  - Apply that over r=0..3 to build the 4×4 result.
