  - The stamp is applied in-place as you scan left-to-right, top-to-bottom. After stamping at the first 5 of a block, the overwritten cells are no longer 5, so subsequent positions within the same block won’t trigger a second stamp. This avoids needing to explicitly detect the top-left of each 5-rectangle.

- Putting it together:
  - Step 1: Find the first non-(0,5) cell and measure the contiguous rectangular template by expanding down and right until hitting 0 or 5.
  - Step 2: Iterate over all top-left candidate positions where the template could fit; if the current cell equals 5, stamp the template values there.
