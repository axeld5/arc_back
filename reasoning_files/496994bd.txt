Here’s how I would solve it:

- Look at the pattern in the examples. The output’s first five rows are identical to the input’s first five rows.
- The output’s last five rows are the reverse of those same first five rows.
- The lower half of the input is ignored entirely; the output is built solely from the top half (which in these examples is exactly five rows).
- Therefore, the transformation is: take the first five rows, then append those five rows in reverse order to form a 10-row result.

Algorithm:
- Treat the input as a list of rows (length 10).
- Extract the first five rows.
- Produce the output as those five rows followed by their reverse.

To organize it:
- Write a helper that, given the full list, takes the first five rows and returns them concatenated with their reversed order.
- The main solver just calls that helper.
